title: "Act on a decision"
description: "An evaluation returns one of three decisions, but only a review has more to say. A clean approval comes back with no reasons and nothing to do."
kind: guide

Branch on `decision`. It is `approve`, `review` or `decline`, and it sits with a `score` from 0 to 1000 where higher is worse. Anton does not return how it reached the decision, so what you act on is the decision, the reasons and the actions.

The result fields exist only once `status` is `completed`. A `payout_screening` evaluation answers `202` with `status: pending` and no result; the rest answer `201` already decided.

## Get a decision

<RunStep title="Evaluate a payee" method="POST" path="/v1/intelligence/evaluations"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "payee-john-doe-001" }}
  body={{
    use_case: "payee_registration",
    entity: {
      type: "person",
      name: "John Doe",
      country: "US"
    }
  }}>
  The `use_case` fixes the required fields. A `payee_registration` needs an `entity.name` or an `entity.vault_token_id`.
</RunStep>

For `payout_screening`, poll [`GET /v1/intelligence/evaluations/{id}`](/reference/intelligence/get-evaluation) until `status` is `completed` or `failed`, or subscribe to `intelligence.evaluation.completed` and `intelligence.evaluation.failed`. A failed evaluation carries `failure_reason: evaluation_failed` and no decision. That is not an approval, so do not treat it as one.

## Branch on the decision

| Decision | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `approve` | Go on. `reasons` and `required_actions` are empty for a clean approval. |
| `review` | The decision is not a plain approval. `reasons` says why and `required_actions` says what you can do. |
| `decline` | Stop. Don't create the payee or send the payout. |

A review in your queue can name the evaluation that opened it in `evaluation_id`. [Work the review queue](/intelligence/reviews) covers it.

`coverage` says how much of the screening panel answered: `complete`, `partial` or `error`. Read it with the decision, not instead of it.

## Read the score and tier

`score` is an integer from 0 to 1000. `tier` is `low`, `medium`, `high` or `critical`. Store both, and branch on `decision` rather than a score threshold of your own.

## Reasons and required actions

| Reason | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `watchlist_review` | A possible or confirmed list match needs review. |
| `identity_unverified` | The identity could not be confirmed. |
| `information_incomplete` | Details needed for a confident answer were missing. |
| `elevated_risk` | The risk is elevated, or the reason is not one of the others. |

| Action | What to do |
|---|---|
| `provide_missing_information` | Resubmit with the missing details. |
| `verify_identity` | Verify the subject's identity with a [verification subject](/concepts/verifications). |

Both lists can gain values. Treat one you do not know as the generic case: `elevated_risk` for a reason, and no action for an unknown action.

## Decisions a person makes later

An evaluation's `decision` is Anton's. When it opens a review, your team records its own decisions on that review: `not_a_match`, `confirmed_match`, `no_concern` and the rest. Those are a separate object, and one your API credential proposes that clears the subject returns `requires_approval: true` with `approval_rule: api_clearance`, so it waits for an independent person. [Record and approve a decision](/intelligence/record-a-decision) covers it.
