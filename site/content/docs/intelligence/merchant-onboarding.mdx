`merchant_onboarding` evaluates a company at the point you would accept it as a customer. The decision is in the response, so you can gate your own signup flow on it.

<BeforeYouBegin items={["Sandbox credentials with the intelligence scope", "The business's legal name, or a vault token that holds it", "A registration number, if you have one"]} />

## Name the business

`entity.type` must be `business`; `person` is a `422`. One of `name`, `vault_token_id` or `document.number` is required. `country` and a `document` of `type` `business_registration` or `tax_id`, with its `country` and `number`, give the screening more to match the company on.

  </Choice>
</Decision>

## Send the evaluation

<RunStep title="Screen the business" method="POST" path="/v1/intelligence/evaluations"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "intel-merchant-example-001" }}
  body={{
    use_case: "merchant_onboarding",
    client_reference_id: "merchant-example-001",
    entity: {
      type: "business",
      reference_id: "merchant-example",
      name: "Example Imports LLC",
      country: "US",
      document: {
        type: "business_registration",
        country: "US",
        number: "TEST-REG-123"
      }
    }
  }}>
  The response is `201` with `status: "completed"`.
</RunStep>

The `merchant` object on the request is accepted but ignored today; don't depend on it. Put your own context in `metadata`.

## Act on the decision

`approve` lets the business through. `decline` stops it. On `review`, `reasons` says why and `required_actions` may ask for `provide_missing_information`, which means resubmit the same business with more details, or `verify_identity`, which points to a [verification subject](/concepts/verifications) for the people behind it. [Decisions](/intelligence/decisions) lists every value.
