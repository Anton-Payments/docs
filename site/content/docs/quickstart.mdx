Each call below leaves something the next one needs: a token, a payee, an account to pay into, money to send, the payout, and a way to hear how it ends. Keep the IDs as you go.

<BeforeYouBegin items={["A sandbox client_id and client_secret from the dashboard", "The DPoP private key the dashboard generated with them", "A shell, or the console on this page"]} />

## Get a token

Trade your `client_id` and `client_secret` for a token. Every call after this one carries that token and a fresh DPoP proof signed for its exact method and URL. [Authentication](/authentication) has a working signer.

<RunStep title="Mint an access token" method="POST" path="/oauth/token"
  headers={{ "Authorization": "Basic $CLIENT_CREDENTIALS", "Content-Type": "application/x-www-form-urlencoded" }}
  body={{ grant_type: "client_credentials" }}>
  A sandbox token lasts 8 hours. There is no refresh token: mint a new one when it runs out.
</RunStep>

## Register who you pay

A payee is identity and nothing else. Its personal details go to a vault on the way in, and you get back a `ben_` ID. Send your own ID as `external_ref`, and build the `Idempotency-Key` from it, so a retry returns the same payee instead of a second one.

<RunStep title="Register the payee" method="POST" path="/v1/beneficiaries"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "ben-contractor-1042" }}
  body={{
    type: "individual",
    country: "GB",
    external_ref: "contractor-1042",
    individual: {
      first_name: "Jane",
      last_name: "Smith",
      email: "jane@example.com",
      phone: "+442079460000",
      birth_date: "1988-05-12",
      nationality: "GB",
      address: { street_line_1: "10 Example Street", city: "London", postal_code: "SW1A 1AA", country: "GB" }
    }
  }}>
  Comes back `active`. [Register a payee](/guides/register-a-payee) covers businesses and duplicates.
</RunStep>

## Give them an account

An instrument is where the money lands, and it belongs to one payee through `beneficiary_id`. The `iban` goes in and never comes back out; reads show a masked display.

<RunStep title="Register the instrument" method="POST" path="/v1/instruments"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "ins-contractor-1042-eur-primary" }}
  body={{
    beneficiary_id: "ben_01HX8Z9K0M2N3P4Q5R6S7T8UA1",
    method: "iban",
    currency: "EUR",
    label: "Primary account",
    default: true,
    credentials: { account_holder_name: "Jane Smith", iban: "DE89370400440532013000" }
  }}>
  [Register an instrument](/guides/register-an-instrument) covers wallets and the other methods.
</RunStep>

## Fund the sandbox

A payout spends a balance you already hold. In the sandbox, one call credits it; in production the money arrives by bank transfer instead, and this endpoint doesn't exist.

<RunStep title="Credit your sandbox balance" method="POST" path="/v1/sandbox/deposits"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "fund-usd-1" }}
  body={{ currency: "USD", amount: "1000.00" }}>
  Deposits stack. [Fund your balance](/guides/fund-your-balance) covers production funding.
</RunStep>

## Send the payout

USD from your balance, EUR to the payee over SEPA. `fixed_side` says the payee gets exactly the `destination_amount`; Anton prices the other side. Amounts are strings.

<RunStep title="Create the payout" method="POST" path="/v1/payouts"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "payout-PO-8821" }}
  body={{
    beneficiary_id: "ben_01HX8Z9K0M2N3P4Q5R6S7T8UA1",
    instrument_id: "ins_01HX8Z9K0M2N3P4Q5R6S7T8UA2",
    source_amount: "550.00",
    source_currency: "USD",
    destination_amount: "500.00",
    destination_currency: "EUR",
    method: "sepa",
    rail_type: "fiat",
    fixed_side: "fixed_destination",
    purpose: "supplier_payment",
    reference: "PO-8821"
  }}>
  The call returns before the money moves. Anton checks the payout first, and everything after arrives as events.
</RunStep>

## Hear how it ends

Polling tells you less, later. Subscribe once and every payout reports its own progress to your URL, signed. Answer any `2xx` quickly and do the work afterwards.

<RunStep title="Create the subscription" method="POST" path="/v1/webhooks"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "webhook-orders-1" }}
  body={{
    url: "https://example.com/hooks",
    events: ["payout.completed", "payout.failed", "payout.returned", "payout.on_hold"]
  }}>
  The signing `secret` is shown once. Store it now.
</RunStep>
