An FX quote is a price, and an exchange is the act of taking it. Together they turn money in one balance into money in another, at a rate you can hold. A payout across currencies doesn't use them: it is priced with a [pricing quote](/guides/pay-across-currencies), and an `fxq_` ID is never valid there.

<BeforeYouBegin items={["A sandbox OAuth client and its DPoP key", "Available balance in the currency you give up", "An account in the currency you receive"]} />

## Name the pair and fix one side

`source_currency` is the currency you give up and `destination_currency` the one you receive. Fix exactly one side with `source_amount` or `destination_amount`, and Anton computes the other. Sending both is a validation error.

Amounts are decimal strings at the currency's precision: two places for USD, none for JPY, six for USDC. More places is `422 amount_precision_invalid`.

You need an account in the destination currency. Without one, an exchange fails with `422 no_buy_account`. [Fund your balance](/guides/fund-your-balance) covers getting one; in the sandbox a deposit creates it.

## Lock the price or only look at it

<Decision>
  <Choice title="Locked" value="locked: true" fields="stored, expires_at, executable" set={{ locked: true }} picked>
    The quote is stored with an `expires_at` and can be executed once at its rate until then. This is the default.
  </Choice>
  <Choice title="Indicative" value="locked: false" fields="price only, expires_at: null, not executable" set={{ locked: false }}>
    A price for display. The rate can move, and executing it fails with `422 quote_not_locked`.
  </Choice>
</Decision>

For a price with no write at all, `GET /v1/fx/rates` takes the same fields as query parameters and returns an indicative quote with `locked: false`. Fix at most one side; with none, you get the price of one unit of the destination currency. Today the list holds the one quote for your pair.

## Request a quote

<RunStep title="Create an FX quote" method="POST" path="/v1/fx/quotes"
  headers={{ "Idempotency-Key": "fxq-A-1042" }}
  body={{
    source_currency: "USD",
    destination_currency: "EUR",
    destination_amount: "500.00",
    locked: true,
    metadata: { order_ref: "A-1042" }
  }}>
  The quote carries both amounts, the `customer_rate` you get, the `mid_market_rate` it was built from, a `fee` and an `expires_at`. A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` and body returns the original quote.
</RunStep>

`markup_basis_points` is the markup on the mid-market rate. A `locked` quote is `active` until it is `used`, `expired` or `cancelled`. An indicative quote is always `active`.

## Execute the exchange

Send the quote's `id` as `quote_id`. A quote executes once, and only before its `expires_at`.

```json POST /v1/fx/exchanges
{
  "quote_id": "fxq_dbvm07aukoh6sl1ll14g",
  "metadata": { "order_ref": "A-1042" }
}
```

To skip the quote, send `source_currency`, `destination_currency` and `destination_amount` instead, and Anton quotes and executes in one step. `quote_id` can't be combined with those fields. The one-step form gives you no look at the rate first, so use it when you don't need one.

The exchange has its own ID, starting `fxx_`, and a `status` of `completed`, `pending` or `failed`. It carries the `quote_id`, both amounts and the rate you got. Fetch it with `GET /v1/fx/exchanges/{id}`, or list your exchanges with `GET /v1/fx/exchanges`, filtered by `status` or by a currency on either side.

## Wait for the event

The outcome reaches you as an event, so you needn't poll. `fx.exchange.created`, `fx.exchange.completed` and `fx.exchange.failed` carry the exchange object, and `fx.quote.created` carries the quote. [Track payouts with webhooks](/guides/track-payouts-with-webhooks) covers subscribing and verifying them.

## When it goes wrong

<Errors>
  <ErrorRow status={404} code="resource_missing">`quote_id` names no quote of yours.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={409} code="quote_already_used">The quote was already executed. Request a new one.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="quote_expired">The quote passed `expires_at`. Request a new one and execute it straight away.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="quote_not_locked">The quote was indicative. Create it with `locked: true`, the default.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="insufficient_balance">The available balance in the source currency can't cover it. Fund it or reduce the amount.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="no_buy_account">You hold no account in the destination currency.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="no_funding_account">On a quote: you hold no account in the source currency.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={422} code="amount_precision_invalid">An amount has more decimal places than its currency allows.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={502} code="fx_rates_unavailable">Rates are unavailable for the moment. Retry with the same `Idempotency-Key`.</ErrorRow>
  <ErrorRow status={503} code="locked_rate_unavailable">A locked rate isn't available right now. Wait for `Retry-After`, then retry.</ErrorRow>
</Errors>

## Idempotency

Quotes and exchanges both require an `Idempotency-Key`. Build it from your own record so a retry after a timeout returns what you already made instead of converting twice. [Idempotency](/concepts/idempotency) has the rest.
