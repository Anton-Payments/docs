A payout is checked before any rail sees it. When a check needs a closer look, the payout becomes `on_hold`. How Anton decides is in [How Anton evaluates payouts](/concepts/how-anton-evaluates-payouts).

<BeforeYouBegin items={["A webhook subscription that includes payout.on_hold and payout.processing", "A pending state in your UI that is not an error"]} />

## Know it has happened

`payout.on_hold` fires when a payout is placed on hold. The payout in `data.object` has `status` `on_hold`, and its `status_message` is the only text Anton gives. A typical one reads "This payout is under review", and nothing follows it.

A hold can also begin after you created the payout and saw `processing`. A payout can go from `created` or `processing` to `on_hold`, so handle the event whenever it arrives, not only in the create response.

## Read the payout

You don't need to poll for this. Read the payout once when you want to confirm what an event told you, or when you are reconciling after downtime.

<RunStep title="Read a held payout" method="GET" path="/v1/payouts/{id}"
  pathParams={{ id: "pay_dbvqrpqukoh470i0a400" }}>
  Look at `status`, `status_message` and `failure_code`. On a held payout `failure_code` is `null`, and so is `risk`.
</RunStep>

The payout's timeline is at [`GET /v1/payouts/{id}/events`](/reference/payouts/list-payout-events), oldest first. It carries each public status the payout has had and no free text, so it cannot tell you why either.

## Show your user a pending state

A held payout is not a failed one. Tell your user the payout is under review and that you will update them. Don't repeat `status_message` as if it were a reason, and don't guess one. Anton doesn't give you a cause, so anything you show would be invented.

## What not to do

- **Don't cancel it.** A held payout cannot be cancelled. The request returns `409 payout_on_hold`.
- **Don't send it again.** A second payout for the same invoice risks paying twice. If your creating call timed out, retry it with the same `Idempotency-Key` and you get the first payout back.
- **Don't treat the hold as a defect to retry around.** There is no flag, header or field that skips or overrides a check.

## How a hold ends

<Lifecycle states="on_hold, processing"
  notes="Under review. Cannot be cancelled | Review ended, payout continues"
  edges="review ends">
  A hold can also end in `cancelled` or `failed`. Both are final.
</Lifecycle>

Release is `payout.processing`, which also fires when a payout is first submitted to the rail. Read `status` in the payload rather than assuming which case it is. A hold that ends in `failed` arrives as `payout.failed`, and `failure_code` can be `cannot_be_processed` with no further detail. [Handle returns and failures](/guides/handle-returns-and-failures) covers that ending.

A review has no published duration. Alert on a payout that stays held longer than your own deadline, and keep your user's pending state honest while it does.

## When your team can help

If an answer from you is what the review needs, it is an RFI, and your team answers it in the dashboard. The API cannot tell you whether a given hold is waiting on one. See [Respond to an RFI](/guides/respond-to-an-rfi).
