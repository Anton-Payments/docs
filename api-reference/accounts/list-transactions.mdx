---
title: "List All Transactions"
openapi: "GET /v1/transactions"
---

<Note>
  Narrow the list with `currency`, `type`, `direction`, `status`, `created_after`
  and `created_before`. For one currency, send `currency=USD`. Timestamps are
  RFC 3339 UTC with milliseconds, such as `2026-11-01T00:00:00.000Z`.
</Note>
