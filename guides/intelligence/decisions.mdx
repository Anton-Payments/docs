---
title: "Decisions and Next Actions"
description: "Understand Intelligence decisions, risk levels, and next actions."
---

Anton Intelligence gathers evidence about the subject and returns one decision. You branch on `decision`, and read `reasons` and `required_actions` to see what to do next.

## Decisions

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `approve` | No meaningful adverse evidence, or the evidence was cleared. |
| `review` | Needs a closer look. `reasons` says why and `required_actions` says what you can do. |
| `decline` | High-confidence adverse evidence. Do not continue the flow. |

## Tiers

`score` runs from 0 to 1000, and higher is worse. `tier` is its band: `low`, `medium`, `high` or `critical`.

## Reasons

| Reason | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `watchlist_review` | A possible or confirmed list match needs review. |
| `identity_unverified` | The identity could not be confirmed. |
| `information_incomplete` | Details needed for a confident answer were missing. |
| `elevated_risk` | The risk is elevated, or the reason is not one of the others. |

## Required actions

| Action | What to do |
|---|---|
| `provide_missing_information` | Resubmit with the missing details. |
| `verify_identity` | Verify the subject's identity with a [verification subject](/guides/verifications). |

Reasons and actions can gain new values. Treat a value you do not know as the generic case.
